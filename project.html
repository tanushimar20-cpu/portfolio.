PROJECT ON DISASTER MANAGEMENT


    Disaster management is the organized process of planning and using resources to deal with emergencies and reduce the damage caused by calamities.
    Disasters are sudden, catastrophic events that cause huge losses to human life, property, and the environment.
    They are broadly grouped into natural events like floods and earthquakes, and man-made events like industrial accidents.Disaster management focuses on lowering the severe impact of these unexpected events rather than completely stopping them.
    Its primary four key phases are mitigation, preparedness, response, and recovery.Mitigation involves taking early steps to lower risks and vulnerabilities before a crisis hits.
    Preparedness includes making emergency plans, conducting safety drills, and training people.
    The response phase focuses on immediate rescue operations, medical aid, and supplying safe food and water.
    The recovery phase helps affected communities rebuild homes, restore infrastructure, and return to normal life.Organizations like the National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) coordinate nationwide plans and safety guidelines in India.
    Public awareness and community education play a vital role in saving lives during emergencies.

CAUSES 

    

    Disasters are caused by a combination of natural forces, climate change, and human activities that turn environmental hazards into destructive events. 
    Natural and Geophysical CausesTectonic movements: Shifting plates in the earth's crust build pressure that releases suddenly as earthquakes or triggers tsunamis and volcanic eruptions.
    Atmospheric and weather shifts: Variations in wind, air pressure, and ocean temperatures feed severe storms, cyclones, and prolonged drought.
    Human-Induced and Environmental FactorsClimate change: Global warming raises ocean and air temperatures, allowing storms to hold more water and intensifying floods, heatwaves, and wildfires.
    Deforestation and soil erosion: Cutting down trees removes natural barriers that absorb groundwater and stabilize soil, leading to worse floods and landslides.
    Poor urban planning: Rapid, unregulated growth and blocked drainage systems increase the risk of severe flash floods in cities.
    Industrial and technological accidents: Human error, negligence, or mechanical failures can cause chemical spills, industrial explosions, or major biological outbreaks.
    You can read more about earth-driven and weather-driven events on Britannica's Natural Disaster Guide. [1] (https://www.britannica.com/science/natural-disaster).

MANAGEMENT

    

    Disaster management is the continuous, systematic process of planning, organizing, coordinating, and implementing measures to prevent, mitigate, prepare for,
     respond to, and recover from natural or man-made disasters
